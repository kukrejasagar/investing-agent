## Method

- **SMA/EMA:** simple and exponential averages of close; EMAs seeded with the SMA of their first N values. MACD = EMA12 − EMA26, signal = 9-period EMA of MACD.
- **RSI:** Wilder's smoothing (first average = simple mean of 14 gains/losses, then (prev×13 + current)/14).
- **Bollinger:** 20-period SMA ± 2 × population standard deviation (ddof = 0). %B = (close − lower)/(upper − lower).
- **Trend:** five checks (close vs SMA20, SMA20 vs SMA50, SMA50 vs SMA100, EMA12 vs EMA26, SMA50 vs its value 10 sessions ago), each +1 bullish / −1 bearish / 0 if the two values are within 0.5% (flat); net score ≥ +3 = up, ≤ −3 = down, otherwise sideways.
- **Volume Delta:** "Volume Delta (Close)" is each day's net delta. Confirmed when the sign of the 10-day close-to-close change matches the sign of the 10-day rolling sum of delta; otherwise diverging.
- **Support candidates** (full series): swing lows (lowest low within ±3 bars); bars whose low came within 0.5% of the lower Bollinger Band; days where at least two of SMA20/50/100 were within 0.5% of each other (level = their mean); volume-delta absorption bars (intrabar low-delta in the most negative 20% of the series, at least half of it bought back by the close, and close in the upper half of the bar's range; level = bar low). Candidates are merged greedily into ±0.5% zones; only zones entirely below the last close are kept. Where two qualifying zones' bands overlap (centres within 1%), only the one with more touches is listed.
- **Backtest:** a touch = prior close above the zone top and the bar's low reaches the zone top (gaps through the zone count). The first close outside the zone on the touch day or the next 3 sessions decides it: above the zone = reversed, below = broke; still inside = unresolved (counted in N, not as a reversal). After a touch, the next one needs a fresh close back above the zone. Touches with fewer than 3 sessions of follow-through at the end of the data are excluded. Zones need N ≥ 2; N < 5 is flagged. Up to 4 zones, nearest to price first.
- **Caveats:** touches are counted across the full series, including touches before the level formed, so rates include look-ahead. Swing-low candidates are reversals by construction, which biases rates upward for zones built mostly from swing lows. Rates on small N are not statistically meaningful.
